BCA Master ("BCA Master", "we", "us", or "our") is an educational mobile application developed by Tech Satyam for BCA students.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the BCA Master application.

By using BCA Master, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About BCA Master

BCA Master is an educational application designed to help Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) students access and organize learning resources.

The application provides features including:

  • BCA Notes
  • Books and study materials
  • Educational PDFs
  • Integrated PDF reading
  • Programming resources
  • Career and learning roadmaps
  • Offline-friendly learning resources
  • Dark mode

BCA Master does not require users to create an account to use its core learning features.

BCA Master does not require users to provide personal information such as their name, phone number, password, or payment information to use the core features of the application.

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we may receive information that you choose to provide, such as your email address, name if included, the contents of your message, and any attachments or other information you voluntarily provide.

We use this information to respond to inquiries, provide support, investigate problems, or improve the application.

The application and third-party services used by the application may automatically process certain technical or device-related information. Depending on the services and configuration used, this may include:

  • Device information

  • Diagnostic information
  • Crash information

Some of this information may be processed by third-party service providers rather than directly by BCA Master.
